The included adopt alternative is chosen explicitly: NSFW, furry, futa, outfit, or another agreed version. At AB, NSFW remains included even when the Tier II choice is different; choosing NSFW does not create two NSFW versions. Extra outfits remain $50 each; a paid futa version is $25 when a NSFW base or version exists, is selected, or is included; otherwise $50. A futa chosen as the included alternative is free. Other additional version prices remain $50. Character-count and prop fees follow the calculator.

Minimum adopt auction bid increment: $5. Tiers are cumulative. PSD only for adopt AB or above, on request. Commercial use is agreed separately.

YCH templates accept repeat orders, with species, gender, body type and template edits agreed before ordering. An exact adopt design sells once; after sale, only a separate similar/twin design enquiry is offered.

Custom/YCH payments: PayPal or Ko-fi. Adopts: PayPal, Ko-fi or DeviantArt, confirmed for the listing. Pay within 48 hours after confirmation. After 24 hours without a reply, an unpaid hold may be released; this releases a YCH reservation rather than selling out its reusable template. Adopt bids over $200 may use a prior agreed payment plan with a required down payment; exact amounts and dates are agreed individually. Final files follow full payment. Work deadlines start after payment and all references, or the agreed first instalment and references.

Adopt resale is at the original paid price or lower; notify the artist and retain design credit. Naming, lore/design edits, posting, gifts and trades are allowed as described in the ToS; commercial rights are separate.

Cancellation/refund arrangements are discussed before payment; the site does not present an unconditional no-refund ban. If the artist cannot complete agreed work, cancellation and refund for the uncompleted part are discussed.
